Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: early brand identity exploration board for a mass-market digital product

Color palette: warm paper off-white, deep ink navy, memory violet, gentle coral, muted sage. Flat colors only.
Text (verbatim): "Mind Diary"
Constraints: render the name exactly once and exactly as written; strong silhouette; balanced negative space; friendly rounded geometry; no gradients; no mockup devices; no 3D; no watermark.
